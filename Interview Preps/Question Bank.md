# Behavioral Question Bank

Grouped by competency. Frequency = how often this appears in tech interviews.

> **Key insight:** one strong "challenge" story, told with the right angle, can answer ~half of these. The buckets overlap by design.

---

## 1. Challenge / Problem-Solving — 🔥 HIGH

Probing: can you handle ambiguity, own a hard problem, and drive it to resolution?

- Tell me about a time you faced a significant challenge and how you resolved it.
- Describe a difficult problem you solved. How did you approach it?
- Tell me about a time you had to solve a problem with little or no guidance.
- Walk me through a time you had to make a decision with incomplete information.
- Tell me about a time something you built didn't work as expected. What did you do?

---

## 2. Failure / Mistake — 🔥 HIGH

Probing: self-awareness, accountability, growth. **You must end with the learning.**

- Tell me about a time you failed.
- Describe a mistake you made and how you handled it.
- Tell me about a project that didn't go well. What was your role in that?
- Tell me about a time you received difficult feedback.
- What's a decision you regret, and what would you do differently?

---

## 3. Conflict / Disagreement — 🔥 HIGH

Probing: emotional maturity, communication, collaboration under tension.

- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a coworker or teammate.
- Describe a conflict you had at work and how you resolved it.
- Tell me about a time you had to deliver difficult feedback.
- Tell me about a time you had to push back on a decision.
- Tell me about a time you worked with a difficult person.

---

## 4. Leadership / Initiative — HIGH

Probing: do you lead without being asked, take ownership, influence others?

- Tell me about a time you led a team or project.
- Describe a time you took initiative on something outside your job description.
- Tell me about a time you had to influence someone without authority.
- Tell me about a time you had to make an unpopular decision.
- Describe a time you mentored or helped a teammate grow.

---

## 5. Teamwork / Collaboration — MEDIUM

- Tell me about a time you worked on a team. What was your role?
- Describe a time you helped a teammate who was struggling.
- Tell me about a time you had to collaborate across teams or functions.
- Tell me about a time the team disagreed on direction. How did you help?

---

## 6. Adaptability / Change — MEDIUM

- Tell me about a time you had to change course quickly.
- Describe a time your priorities shifted suddenly. How did you respond?
- Tell me about a time you had to learn something new fast.
- Tell me about a time you had to work in an environment of rapid change.

---

## 7. Prioritization / Time Management — MEDIUM

- Tell me about a time you had too much on your plate.
- Describe a time you had competing deadlines. How did you decide what to do first?
- Tell me about a time you missed a deadline. What happened?
- Tell me about a time you had to say "no" to something.

---

## 8. Achievement / Motivation — LOW–MEDIUM

- Tell me about your proudest accomplishment.
- What are you most proud of in your career so far?
- Tell me about a time you exceeded expectations.
- What achievement are you most proud of, and why?

---

## How these map to Amazon's Leadership Principles

Most tech behavioral questions are Amazon principles in disguise. Prep these and you cover most of the field:

| Principle | Bucket above |
|---|---|
| Ownership / Bias for Action / Deliver Results | Challenge, Achievement |
| Have Backbone; Disagree & Commit | Conflict |
| Earn Trust | Conflict, Teamwork |
| Dive Deep | Challenge (problem-solving) |
| Learn and Be Curious | Adaptability, Failure |
| Insist on the Highest Standards | Failure, Achievement |
| Customer Obsession | Achievement, Challenge |
