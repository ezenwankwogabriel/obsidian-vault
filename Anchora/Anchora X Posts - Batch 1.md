# Anchora — Today's Post (Aug 4)

**THE POST:**

I spent last week trying to calculate my net worth.

Eight apps. One abandoned spreadsheet. A lot of staring at my phone.

That's literally how Anchora started. I wanted one place to see everything I own — bank accounts, crypto, the land papers in my cabinet, insurance policies, business accounts. Just one dashboard.

Turns out nobody had built that for Nigeria. So I'm building it.

The core is simple: log your assets, see your full picture in one view. Category breakdown, portfolio estimate, everything.

And since the data is there anyway, it serves a second purpose — your trusted person gets access if you can't hand it over yourself. But that's the bonus. The main thing is just knowing what you have, in one place.

Still building. Still shipping. Dashboard works, onboarding flow is coming together.
