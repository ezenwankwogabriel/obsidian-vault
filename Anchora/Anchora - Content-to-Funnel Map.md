# Anchora — Content-to-Funnel Map

**Purpose:** Map every piece of content to a funnel stage and a signup prompt, so content drives *adoption* — not just awareness. Companion to [[Anchora - Content Marketer Role & Hiring]].

**Core mechanic:** Lead with the **living benefit** (organize where your financial and physical assets are held — today). The legacy question ("what happens when you're gone") is the *reveal* that deepens urgency — never the headline.

**Compliance guardrail (applies to every piece below):** No "estate," "beneficiary," "executor," "recovery" (user-facing), "manage," "credential handover," "financial planning," "wealth management," "asset transfer," or "outcome guarantees." Correct label: **Trusted Contact**.

---

## 1. The funnel (5 stages)

```
ATTENTION ──► INTEREST ──► CONSIDERATION ──► CONVERSION ──► RETENTION / REFERRAL
 (they see     (they feel    (they believe      (they sign       (living value
  the problem)  the living    it works)          up / activate)   deepens + they
                benefit)                                              add a contact)
```

| Stage | Question in the audience's head | What content must answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Attention** | "Do I even have this problem?" | Yes, and it's closer than you think |
| **Interest** | "What do I get *right now*?" | A living record of everything you hold — organized today |
| **Consideration** | "Does this actually work / can I trust it?" | Proof: founder transparency, product progress, real use |
| **Conversion** | "Why should I start *today*?" | Low-friction signup, clear first action |
| **Retention/Referral** | "Was this worth it? Who else needs this?" | The living dashboard keeps delivering value → they name a Trusted Contact |

## 3. The reveal mechanic (how legacy content resolves to a living action)

Problem Education content is allowed to *open* the loop with the uncomfortable question — but it must always *close* on a present-tense action.

```
OPEN (problem):  "You don't actually know where everything is, do you?
                  When was the last time you saw all your accounts in one place?"

BUILD (tension): "Now ask the harder question: if you weren't around tomorrow,
                  would anyone know where to start looking?"

RESOLVE (living):"You don't need a will to fix the first problem today.
                  Anchora gives you one clear map of everything you hold —
                  and lets you name one Trusted Person to guide your people later."

CTA: "Start your free record map."
```

The legacy question *raises the stakes*; the living benefit *sells the product*. If the legacy question is the headline, you've lost the adoption play.

## 4. Worked examples — one full piece per pillar

### Pillar 1 — Living Value Dashboard (Interest)

**Format:** X thread + Instagram carousel ("Where is your money, actually?")

```
Tweet 1: "Quick test: without opening any app, list every financial account
         you own and where it's held. Banks. Pension. Investments. Insurances.

         Most people can't. Not because they're careless — because nothing
         gives them one place to see it all."

Tweet 2: "That's the living problem Anchora solves first: one clear map of
         what you hold and where. Not a will. Not a finance app. A record
         of your financial and physical life, organized."

Tweet 3: "And when you name a Trusted Contact, that map stops being just
         yours — it's the starting point your people would need."

CTA: "Build your map free — 3 records, no card. anchora.com.ng"
```
*Metric:* thread → profile → signup → first record created (within 24h).

### Pillar 2 — Problem Education (Attention)

**Format:** SEO blog + 30s short video ("What happens to your accounts when you're not around?")

**Blog outline:**
1. The uncomfortable reality: most Nigerian families can't locate a loved one's accounts after they pass (banks, PFA, investments, insurance).
2. Why it's harder here: next-of-kin isn't a legal shortcut to access — each institution has its own process.
3. What actually helps: knowing *where* things are held, in one place, with a person who's been told what to do.
4. The living fix: organize the map *now*, name a Trusted Contact *now* — because the guidance only works if the map exists first.

**CTA (end of post):** "You can build that map today. Free to start. anchora.com.ng"
*Metric:* organic search → blog → signup-source = "blog / organic."

### Pillar 3 — Founder Building (Consideration/trust)

### Pillar 4 — Product Updates (Conversion)

*Metric:* feature post → activation (Trusted Contact named).

## 5. Distribution (Nigeria-first)

| Channel | Role | Content type |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp** | The share surface (primary) | Everything must be screenshot/forward-able; every long piece gets a 30s WhatsApp-ready summary |
| **X/Twitter** | Founder + problem education + product updates | Threads, build-in-public |
| **Instagram/TikTok** | Problem education + living value (short-form) | 30–45s vertical explainers |
| **SEO blog** | Problem education (compounding) | Long-form guides on the institutional-process moat |
| **YouTube (later)** | Authority + living value deep-dives | 5–10 min walkthroughs |

**Rule:** Produce one long-form piece weekly (SEO anchor), then derive short-form + WhatsApp summaries from it. Never the reverse.

## 6. Measurement (past vanity metrics)

**North-star:** activated signups (account created **and** first record created) per week, with signup-source attached.

| Leading indicator | Tells you |
|---|---|
| CTA click-through (per piece) | Whether the prompt is compelling |
| Signup-source by piece | Which pillar/content actually converts |
| Activation rate (signup → first record) | Whether onboarding delivers on the living promise |
| Time-to-first-record | Whether the living benefit is real, not just claimed |
| Search impressions → clicks on problem-education | Whether the moat content is findable |

**Weekly report (the hire delivers):** signups by source, best-converting piece, one pillar to double down on, one to cut.

**Kill rule:** a piece/pillar that produces reach but zero signup-source movement for 3 weeks gets cut — regardless of impressions.

## 8. Guardrails recap (for the hire's cheat sheet)

1. **Living benefit is the headline; legacy is the reveal.**
2. **Banned words:** estate, beneficiary, executor, recovery (user-facing), manage, credential handover, financial planning, wealth management, asset transfer.
3. **Correct label:** Trusted Contact (or Trusted Person).
4. **No outcome guarantees** — Anchora records and guides; it does not manage, transfer, or appraise.
5. **Product truth only** — never describe unshipped features as live (free tier = 3 records; PRO "coming soon").
