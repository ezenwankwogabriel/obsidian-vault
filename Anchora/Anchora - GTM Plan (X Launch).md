# Anchora — Go to Market: X/Twitter Launch Plan

**Goal:** Build pre-launch awareness + trust for Anchora in the Nigerian market using text-only content. Zero design budget. Zero content team.

**Strategy:** Lead with the **living benefit** — one clear map of where your financial and physical assets are held, organized today. The legacy question ("what happens when you're gone") is the *reveal* that raises the stakes, never the headline. Every post is a brick in two pillars — (1) *this problem is real and fixable today* and (2) *we're building the solution transparently.*

## Cadence

**3 posts per week** (every 2 days). Delivered via Hermes cron — you get a ready-to-copy thread + brief instructions each delivery day.

## Content Pillars

| Pillar | Purpose | % of posts |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Living Value Dashboard** | Sell the present-tense benefit: organize where everything is held, today | 35% |
| **Problem Education** | Make people feel the pain of NOT having a record of their financial/physical life | 25% |
| **Founder Building** | Show the human building it — builds trust in a trust product | 25% |
| **Product Updates** | Show progress, build anticipation | 15% |

> Note: "Market/Industry Insight" is no longer a standalone pillar — it folds into Problem Education and Living Value as supporting evidence.

## Phase Plan

### Phase 1: Pre-Beta (weeks 1–4) — Living Value + Founder Story
No product yet. Lead with the living benefit (the "where is everything, actually?" test) and the founder story. Open the legacy loop but always close on a present-tense action.

### Phase 2: Beta (weeks 5–8) — User Stories + Iteration
First users onboard. Share their reactions (anonymized), fixes you made, screenshots of the living dashboard in use.

### Phase 3: Launch (weeks 9–12) — Push + Proof
Feature threads, pricing rationale, testimonials, comparisons. Lean into Living Value Dashboard walkthroughs as the primary conversion content.

## Delivery Format

Each cron delivery contains:

1. **Pillar & Phase** — which content bucket this post belongs to
2. **The Post** — full draft, ready to copy-paste to X. If it's a thread, each tweet is clearly separated (Tweet 1 / Tweet 2 / ...).
3. **Steps** — exactly what to do (post it, reply to comments, engage with X accounts)
4. **Optional variant** — an alternative angle if the main one doesn't resonate

## How to Use Each Delivery

1. Open X/Twitter
2. Copy the post draft
3. Post it
4. Spend 10 minutes replying to comments and engaging with replies
5. Done. That's the core work for that day.

## Compliance Guardrails (every post)

- Lead with living benefit; legacy is the reveal.
- Banned words: estate, beneficiary, executor, recovery (user-facing), manage, credential handover, financial planning, wealth management, asset transfer.
- Correct label: **Trusted Contact** (or Trusted Person).
- No outcome guarantees — Anchora records and guides; it does not manage, transfer, or appraise.
- Product truth only — free tier = 3 records; PRO "coming soon."
