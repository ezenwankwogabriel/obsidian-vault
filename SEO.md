Search Engine Optimization or as may now be seen, Search Everywhere Optimization

This is getting your website to show up wherever people search for things related to your biz or the solution you provide.

Google search uses
- Crawl: google sends automated bots to read pages on the internet
- Index: storing the searches from the crawled search
- Rank: looks through index and picks most relevant, and puts them in an other (our goal here)

The questions to ask:
- what are people actually searching for?
- what do they want when they search for it?

## Search Intent
How do you check for intent. you google your keyword, and look at whats ranking

Google gives you a afirst 10 results

Your job is to match that intent and do it like nobody else

SERP: Search Engine Result Page. 
Every keyword has their own.
The shorter the keyword, the lesser useful it is

Intent in the AI era
Keywords: the phrase people type into the search box. figuring out which keywords matter
4 attributes for a keyword
- demand: are people looking for this
- fit: where does the keyword fit in your funnel (is it relevant to the audience)
- intent: what does the searcher want (can you satisfy that)
- Difficulty: can you realistically rank for this given your sites position (can't outrank amazon by default for some keywords)
