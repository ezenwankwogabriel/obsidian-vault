## Week 13: Create Your Signature Framework

This is the single most valuable asset you'll build. A named, repeatable framework that's uniquely yours.

**Formula:** "The [Number or Name] [Noun] Framework"

**Examples of what this looks like:**
- "The Trust-First Hiring Framework"
- "The 3-Core Model for AI Readiness"
- "The 5 Signals of Organizational Decay"

**How to build it (2-hour workshop with yourself):**

1. List every problem you've solved in your career
2. Circle the 2-3 that repeat across clients/roles/industries
3. Identify the common pattern — what do you do first? Second? Third?
4. Name the steps. Give them memorable labels.
5. Draw it. Even badly. A simple visual is worth 10,000 words.
6. Test it: can you explain it to someone in 60 seconds and they get it?

**Publish it as a carousel first.** Then write the definitive article. Then reference it in every subsequent post.

## Week 14-16: Strategic Outreach Sprint

### The Target List

Identify 20 people. Use these filters:
- Active on LinkedIn (posts weekly)
- 2,000–50,000 followers (not too big to notice you)
- In your broader domain (not direct competitors)
- You genuinely respect their work

### The 6-Week Engagement Playbook

| Week | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Comment meaningfully on 2 posts from each person (total 40 comments). No DMs. |
| 2 | Tag 2 of them in a post referencing their work positively. Continue commenting. |
| 3 | Send 5 cold DMs. Use this exact template. |
| 4 | Follow up on non-responders. Start conversations with responders. |
| 5 | Offer something of value to 3 people — share their work, intro them to someone, send them a useful resource. No ask. |
| 6 | For the 3-5 warmest relationships: propose a collaboration. Joint article, live stream, podcast swap, or research piece. |

### DM Template (exact words)

```
Hey [Name] —

I've been following your work on [specific topic they post about] and [one genuine compliment — reference a specific post or idea].

I'm doing some work on [your thesis/pillar] and I'd love to hear your take on [one specific, interesting question].

[Question — 1-2 sentences, open-ended, not a yes/no]

No pressure to reply if you're swamped. Just wanted to connect.

[Your name]
```

## Week 25-30: Add Video

Pick ONE format. Don't try both.

### Format A: Talking-head (2-3 minutes)

- Script: Take your best-performing text post. Expand it into 3 bullet points.
- Record: Phone camera, eye level, good lighting (window light is free).
- Edit: CapCut (free). Trim, add captions, done.
- Post: LinkedIn native video. NOT a YouTube link (60% reach penalty).

### Format B: Screen-share (3-5 minutes)

- Topic: Walk through a tool, a process, or a framework visually.
- Record: Loom or OBS (both free).
- Post: Same — native upload only.

**Target: 1 video per week.** Alternate with your text posts.

## Week 33-36: Monetize the Authority

You've been building for 8+ months. Now turn attention into income.

### Option A: Advisory / Consulting

1. Write a post: "I'm opening 5 spots for [advisory/consulting] in [your domain]."
2. Link to a simple Calendly booking page with a paid option.
3. Pricing: Start at $200-500/hr. Raise after you're booked.
4. Deliver 3-5 sessions. Ask for LinkedIn recommendations after each.

### Option B: Paid Workshop or Course Beta

1. Pick ONE specific problem from your framework.
2. Design a 2-hour live workshop that solves it.
3. Price: $50-150/person. Cap at 20 people.
4. Run it once. Record it. Get testimonials. Iterate.

### Option C: Paid Community or Newsletter

1. Upgrade your Substack: free posts continue, but add a paid tier.
2. Paid gets: 1 extra deep-dive per week + monthly AMA + resource library.
3. Price: $10-15/month.
4. Announce: "I'm adding a paid tier for those who want to go deeper. Free stays free."

## Week 37-40: Your First Conference Talk

Deliver what you pitched in Month 7.

**Preparation checklist:**
- Deck: 20 slides max. One idea per slide. Minimal text.
- Rehearse: 5 full run-throughs. Record yourself. Watch it back.
- Backup plan: Wifi dies? Slides fail? Know your talk well enough to deliver without visuals.
- Capture: Ask someone to record. Post the video everywhere after.

**Network at the event:**
- Before: Post "I'm speaking at [event]. Who's going? Let's meet."
- During: Talk to 5 new people. Not to pitch — to learn what they're working on.
- After: Connect on LinkedIn within 24 hours. Reference something specific from your conversation.
